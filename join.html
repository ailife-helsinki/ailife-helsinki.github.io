Practical information

How to attend

AiLIFE seminars are free and open to everyone: students, researchers, clinicians and anyone curious about AI in the life sciences. No registration is needed for on-site attendance.

On site in Meilahti

Biomedicum Helsinki 1, seminar rooms
Haartmaninkatu 8, 00290 Helsinki

Biomedicum sits in the middle of the Meilahti hospital campus. Trams 4 and 10 and several buses stop at Meilahden sairaala (Meilahti Hospital). The seminar rooms are on the ground floor of Biomedicum 1 and fully accessible; the room for each session is given on the programme, and the lobby information desk can point you to it.

Open in Google Maps

Coffee is served before the talk, courtesy of HiLIFE and FIMM. Come early and meet the community.

On site in Viikki

Some sessions will be held on the Viikki campus so that colleagues from both campuses can take part in person. The room is announced with each seminar on the programme.

Viikki campus map

Online on Zoom

Every seminar is hybrid.

We encourage you to join in person when you can: the discussion, the AiLIFE Radar and the coffee break are where the community forms. For remote speakers, we still gather on site and join the talk together.

Never miss a seminar

  • Subscribe to the AiLIFE calendar (.ics works with Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar). To keep it updated automatically, add this subscription address to your calendar app: . The next seminar is on .
  • Join the mailing list to receive the Zoom link and a reminder before each session.
  • Bookmark the programme; speakers and titles are added as they are confirmed.

For speakers

Talks are 35–40 minutes plus discussion, aimed at a broad life-science audience: please assume curiosity about AI but no machine-learning background. We are happy to host remote talks over Zoom. Please let us know in advance whether we may record the talk and publish the slides on this site.
