AiLIFE: Accelerating life sciences with AI and agents

From literature analysis and experiment planning to data analysis, molecular design, image analysis and automated labs, AI is reshaping how life science is done. AiLIFE brings life scientists and AI researchers together to explore what works, what doesn't yet, and what comes next.

Expect practical talks from local and international speakers, live demonstrations and open discussion. Bring your questions, share your own workflows, and suggest topics and speakers. Whether AI is already part of your research or you're wondering how it could be, join us and help shape this growing community.

Tuesdays 13:00–14:00 · Biomedicum Helsinki & Zoom

What we talk about

Where AI meets the life sciences

  1. 01

    AI agents

    Systems that plan, call tools and act: from literature-scale reasoning to fully automated agentic workflows.

  2. 02

    Foundation models for biology

    Large models of genomes, proteins, cells and images to help us predict, design and interpret biological systems.

  3. 03

    AI-guided experiments

    Automating the loop between hypotheses, experiments and data interpretation.

  4. 04

    Automated laboratories

    Self-driving labs and robotics — the practical challenges of bringing autonomous agents into the wet lab and the clinic

Organized and supported by

AiLIFE is organized by the Machine Learning in Biomedicine, Precision Systems Medicine and Gene Evolution groups, and the HiLIFE Biodata Analytics unit. The seminar series is supported by the Institute for Molecular Medicine Finland (FIMM), the Institute of Biotechnology, and the Helsinki Institute of Life Science (HiLIFE), University of Helsinki.
