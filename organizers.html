Hosting institutions

The series is hosted by the MLBioMed (Machine Learning in Biomedicine) group at FIMM, and is funded by the Helsinki Institute of Life Science (HiLIFE) and the Institute for Molecular Medicine Finland (FIMM), University of Helsinki.

HiLIFE is one of the leading life science research institutes in the Nordics, bridging the University's life-science units and campuses. FIMM is a translational research institute focusing on human genomics and precision medicine, part of HiLIFE and of the Nordic EMBL Partnership for Molecular Medicine.
